# Tara Screen-by-Screen Rollout

Phase 8 turns the token system and shared component layer into page-level behavior.

## Principle

Each screen should feel like part of the same world, but with a different emotional volume.

- Landing: strongest storytelling
- Auth: immersive, but form-friendly
- Onboarding: elegant and guided
- Discover: atmosphere around the card, not inside it
- Matches: soft, readable, warm
- Chat list: minimal and intimate
- Chat detail: content-first with light mood
- Kundali: the strongest mystical space after landing
- Profile: personal and warm
- Settings: the cleanest and least decorative

## Screen Notes

### Landing

- Use the boldest hero treatment.
- Keep one clear emotional message.
- Let the atmosphere lead, then support it with calm feature cards and strong CTAs.

### Auth

- Keep the world visible, but never let it compete with the form.
- Group each step into one clear readable panel.
- Maintain obvious progress and hierarchy.

### Onboarding

- Use one elegant surface instead of many decorative blocks.
- Prioritize clarity, comfort, and one-handed use.
- The continue action should always feel stable and easy to find.

### Discover

- Keep the stage atmospheric.
- Keep the swipe card itself readable and photo-first.
- Controls should feel expressive, but not noisy.

### Matches

- Use softer surfaces than Discover.
- Make the list feel warm and easy to skim.
- Compatibility should read as supporting information, not the loudest thing on screen.

### Chat List

- Keep it minimal and calm.
- Search, rows, and timestamps should feel light and orderly.
- The conversation list should feel quieter than Discover and Matches.

### Chat Detail

- The relationship context belongs in the header.
- The message area should stay the cleanest surface in the app.
- Game controls and composer should feel integrated, not bolted on.

### Kundali

- This is the richest mystical screen after Landing.
- Use a strong hero and strong hierarchy, but keep detail panels readable.
- Editing birth details should feel secondary to the reading itself.

### Profile

- The hero should feel personal and reassuring.
- Metrics, bio, photos, and trait rows should feel warm and grounded.
- Action areas should feel intentional, not admin-like.

### Settings

- Keep it useful and plainspoken.
- Use the same system, but lower the decorative intensity.
- Readability and control confidence matter more than mood here.

## Guardrails

- No dense screen should rely on decorative backgrounds for meaning.
- Day and night should both feel intentional.
- Reusable surfaces should use theme tokens, not hardcoded one-off colors.
- Motion should stay supportive and never become the main event.
