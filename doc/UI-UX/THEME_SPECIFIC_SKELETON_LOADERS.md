# Theme-Specific Skeleton Loaders

## Goal
Loading states should feel like the screen they belong to, not like generic gray blocks.

Every skeleton should:

- match the final screen shape closely
- shimmer softly instead of flashing harshly
- glow subtly in night mode
- feel lightly sunlit in day mode

## Shared Rule
- use the real surface geometry of the destination screen
- keep the atmosphere in the background
- keep the shimmer gentle and readable
- avoid large blank slabs when a more faithful shape is possible

## Screen Loaders

### Discovery
- mirrors the swipe card stage
- keeps the large media block, chips, score pill, and content stack
- night leans violet and moonlit
- day leans sky-blue and gold

### Matches
- mirrors the featured row and the full match list
- keeps avatar circles, chips, and readable row proportions
- mood is softer and more relational than discovery

### Chat List
- mirrors the conversation rows closely
- keeps avatars, preview lines, and timestamp shape
- atmosphere stays minimal and quiet

### Chat Thread
- mirrors incoming/outgoing bubbles and the game card pattern
- keeps the thread intimate and content-first
- avoids aggressive shimmer inside message areas

### Profile
- mirrors the hero card, stat row, about block, photo grid, and profile rows
- loading should still feel personal and warm

### Kundali
- mirrors the summary card, six exploration cards, and opened detail area
- strongest mystical skeleton after landing
- should imply structure without feeling like a fake chart

## Motion Notes
- shimmer sweep should stay smooth and low-contrast
- night mode shimmer should feel like moonlight passing over glass
- day mode shimmer should feel like sunlight crossing a polished surface
- never use stark white bars against dark surfaces
