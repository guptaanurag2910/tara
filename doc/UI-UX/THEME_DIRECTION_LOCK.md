# Tara Theme Direction Lock

## Phase 1 Goal
Lock the emotional and visual direction for Tara before implementation begins.

This document finalizes:
- the two theme moods
- how literal the visual storytelling gets
- where illustration is allowed
- where illustration is restricted
- the main design rule for the whole product

---

## Theme Positioning

Tara should not feel like a normal light mode / dark mode toggle.

It should feel like the same romantic world seen at two different times of day:
- `Night` is for intimacy, reflection, and emotional depth
- `Day` is for warmth, hope, and new beginnings

The product mood should move from:
- `sunlit possibility` in the day
- to `starlit intimacy` at night

This is not a cartoon sky theme.
This is a premium, atmospheric dating product with celestial storytelling.

---

## Final Mood Lock

### Night Mode
**Mood**
- starlit
- intimate
- mystical
- private
- emotionally deep
- quietly magnetic

**Visual feeling**
- deep indigo and midnight navy backgrounds
- moon glow
- small stars and faint constellations
- soft aurora gold accents
- layered shadow and glow instead of flat darkness

**Emotional reading**
- this is the mode for meaningful chats, longing, vulnerability, and depth

### Day Mode
**Mood**
- sunlit
- airy
- hopeful
- open
- warm
- emotionally fresh

**Visual feeling**
- pale sky tones
- warm cream surfaces
- soft gold sunlight
- cloud-white highlights
- breathable spacing and lighter shadow depth

**Emotional reading**
- this is the mode for discovery, optimism, light flirting, and new energy

---

## Literalness Decision

The theme should be **lightly symbolic**, not heavily illustrated.

### Approved Night Motifs
- moon
- stars
- faint constellation lines
- soft cosmic dust
- owl silhouette used very sparingly

### Approved Day Motifs
- sun glow
- clouds
- soft morning haze
- sunrise bird silhouette used very sparingly

### Important Clarification
The day theme should **not** use a loud or cartoon-style rooster/cock illustration.
If that reference is used, it should appear only as a refined sunrise-bird silhouette in hero or empty-state artwork.

### Literalness Level
- `Background`: atmospheric and symbolic
- `Surface UI`: mostly clean and modern
- `Illustration`: reserved, sparse, and elegant

---

## Illustration Allowed

Illustration and decorative world-building are allowed in:
- landing page
- auth page
- empty states
- kundali screen
- selective page headers
- theme transition moments

These are the places where atmosphere helps the product identity most.

---

## Illustration Restricted

Illustration should be minimal or avoided in:
- dense forms
- onboarding input-heavy steps
- chat body
- settings
- long data lists
- profile edit flows
- tables, metrics, or technical chart details

These screens should stay content-first and easy to scan.

---

## Main Visual Rule

**Atmosphere in the background, readability in the foreground.**

This is the core rule for the whole design system.

That means:
- backgrounds carry the mood
- surfaces carry the content
- illustration supports the experience
- decoration never competes with the user task

If a visual element makes the content harder to read, it does not belong.

---

## Composition Rules

### Background Layer
Can include:
- gradients
- glow
- stars
- clouds
- ambient particles
- faint silhouettes

### Content Layer
Must remain:
- readable
- high-contrast
- touch-friendly
- uncluttered
- stable on mobile

### Motion Layer
Should feel like:
- drifting
- twinkling
- glowing
- breathing

It should not feel like:
- bouncing
- flashing
- spinning
- constant visual noise

## Product Interpretation By Screen

### Landing
Strongest theme storytelling.
This is where the world of Tara can feel most cinematic.

### Auth
Immersive but still clean enough for phone, OTP, and password entry.

### Onboarding
Elegant and atmospheric, but more restrained than landing.

### Discover
Mood around the swipe card, not inside the content.

### Matches
Warm and premium, but list readability stays first.

### Chat
Emotionally intimate, but minimal.
The conversation itself is the hero.

### Kundali
Most mystical product screen after landing.
This is the right place for stronger celestial atmosphere.

### Profile
Personal and polished.
The theme should feel like identity, not decoration.

### Settings
Least decorative screen.
Clarity wins.

---

## Tone Guardrails

Tara should feel:
- premium
- poetic
- romantic
- modern
- calm
- emotionally intelligent

Tara should not feel:
- childish
- cartoon-heavy
- fantasy-game-like
- visually noisy
- generic purple astrology UI

---

## Final Approval Decisions

### Approved
- Night = starlit, intimate, mystical
- Day = sunlit, airy, hopeful
- Moon/stars/owl language for night
- Sun/clouds/bird language for day
- Illustration allowed on landing, auth, empty states, kundali, and selective headers
- Illustration restricted on dense utility screens
- Atmosphere in background, readability in foreground

### Not Approved
- cartoon sky treatment
- heavy decorative animals across all screens
- decoration inside dense form and chat content areas
- literal mascot-style rooster artwork
- visually noisy overlays on cards or text

---

## Phase 1 Exit Criteria

Phase 1 is complete when the team agrees that:
1. both moods are emotionally distinct
2. literal motif usage is limited and tasteful
3. illustration boundaries are clear
4. the foreground/background rule is accepted
5. future implementation can proceed without re-debating the theme
